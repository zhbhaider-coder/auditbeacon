# AuditBeacon — Earnings Notes

Free SEO/AEO/GEO page auditor. Free forever, no account, no scan limits —
that's the wedge against SAGE ($9.99) and the other paid auditors.

## Honest timeline
- Day one: $0. Like the other clones, it earns nothing without users.
- The asset is the tool + the traffic it can attract, not immediate revenue.

## Realistic paths (in order)
1. **Freemium later** — keep the core audit free; a Pro tier could add:
   scheduled re-audits, multi-page site crawls, white-label PDF reports,
   historical score tracking. Needs accounts + payments first
   (Stripe isn't direct for Pakistan — Paddle/Lemon Squeezy are realistic).
2. **Affiliate** — "fix this" links can recommend hosting/SEO tools with
   affiliate programs (only where genuinely relevant).
3. **Lead gen** — the audit is a natural top-of-funnel for his $25/$10
   freelance services: "your page scored 61 — I fix this."
4. **AdSense** — needs a content-rich site around it + approval + traffic;
   slowest path.

## What it needs first
Users. Distribution: Product Hunt (~5 Nov when the account unlocks),
Indie Hackers, Uneed, Reddit (aged account), the SEO pages already ranking.
